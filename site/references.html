Experience

Where the thinking comes from.

Behind Axomis Labs sits more than a decade of system and database architecture, implementation and project management in the pharmaceutical industry — an environment where a system isn't considered working until it has been proven to work, and where every change carries documentation and accountability.

Alongside that, years of computerised systems validation: making systems run seamlessly while satisfying every compliance constraint around them. It teaches a particular habit — designing for evidence, not only for function.

Less obviously, twenty-five years of sport across very different disciplines — combat sports, ball sports, volleyball, dance, diving. What carries into the work isn't the sport but the habit behind it: focus, discipline, and knowing what it takes to get good at something unfamiliar. It is also why the industry isn't the point — what transfers is the discipline, not the domain.

  • Regulated systems

    10+ years of architecture, implementation and project management in pharma, where correctness is a requirement rather than an aspiration.

  • Validation & compliance

    Computerised systems validation — building systems that run seamlessly and can still be evidenced against the rules that govern them.

  • Focus & discipline

    Twenty-five years of sport across unrelated disciplines. What transfers is the focus, and the discipline to stay with something until it is genuinely good.

The approach isn't tied to one sector. It transfers wherever systems have to be made to work and stand up to scrutiny — pharmaceutical and other regulated environments included.

Consulting

Work that starts before the software does.

From a single application to a company-wide step up in digitalisation — scoped to whatever the business actually needs next.

  • Project & organisation management

    Structuring work so it can be planned, delegated and finished — with decisions prepared, documented and open to review later.

  • Data & process management

    Getting data into one dependable place and the processes around it into a shape that holds, from industrial operations through to sports applications.

  • Product management

    Deciding what to build, in what order, and what to leave out — then staying with it through delivery rather than handing over a specification.

  • Implementation

    Taking a system from requirements engineering through testing to the production handshake, and staying with it until it runs. Shaped by regulated work, where nothing goes live without the evidence to support it.

  • Software selection

    Comparing what's on the market against what you need, so you buy the right thing — or find out early that building is the better answer.

    For smaller companies wanting to move faster: starting with a single application and going as far as reworking revenue streams through digitalisation and optimisation.

Engagements are shaped to fit — a defined project, ongoing support, or a bespoke arrangement where that suits both sides better.
